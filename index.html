    about our comfort food

    Comfort food is special because it reminds us of happy memories. San² and Cristine love foods such as pizza, fried chicken, burgers, and ice cream because they make them feel relaxed and happy.

    Comfort food refers to foods that bring happiness, warmth, and a sense of comfort. These foods are often connected to special memories, family gatherings, and enjoyable moments with friends. For San² and Cristine, comfort food is more than just something to eat—it is a source of joy and relaxation after a busy day. Foods such as pizza, burgers, fried chicken, pasta, and ice cream remind them of fun experiences and celebrations. Comfort food can help reduce stress, improve one's mood, and create a feeling of satisfaction. Sharing comfort food with loved ones strengthens friendships and creates lasting memories that will always be treasured.
